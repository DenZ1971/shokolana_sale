Production equipment

Main equipment: WALLI-X5

The key production asset is WALLI-X5, a specialized machine for producing chocolate letters and applying chocolate designs to small chocolate bars.

What is it designed for?

WALLi-X5 is a specialized machine designed to produce chocolate letters. It comes with software and can apply a wide range of images from Shocolana's current product catalog to chocolate bars.

How WALLI-X5 works

WALLI-X5 works with a dedicated “Drawing Plan” software module. The operator sees the day's production assignment: which chocolate letters or symbols must be produced and in what quantities.

The assignment is generated from customer orders and current stocks of finished chocolate letters. The operator prepares the chocolate, adds food coloring if required, fills the heated syringe pens with chocolate, and installs them in the machine.

After the required symbol is selected on the touchscreen, the machine loads its drawing program into memory. The operator presses start, and the machine automatically applies the selected symbol to the chocolate bar.

Direct chocolate printing technology

WALLI-X5 does not work like a conventional printer or print pixels. Instead, a fine needle follows a programmed path, forming a raised chocolate line approximately 1 mm thick and filling the outline with chocolate as it moves.

Chocolate is fed from syringe pens by compressed air pressure. The syringe pens are heated to keep the chocolate fluid during operation.

Final quality depends on needle diameter, pressure accuracy, the gap between the needle and chocolate surface, room temperature, and chocolate viscosity. Production know-how and correct setup are therefore critical to consistent results.

  • Power supply: 220 V

ShokoVanna-4L

Chocolate bath

Designed to keep chocolate in working condition during production. Used to melt chocolate and stabilize its temperature.

  • Working capacity: up to 3 kg of chocolate
  • Temperature accuracy: 0.1°C
  • Power supply: 220 V
  • Heater: low-inertia

Vibrolan Bullet

Professional vibrating table

Used to remove air bubbles from chocolate after it is poured into molds. Produces a higher-quality surface on finished products.

  • Purpose: air bubble removal
  • Suitable for: plastic and silicone molds
  • Benefit: reduced product defects
  • Design: compact tabletop unit

ShokoVanna-EZT

Cocoa butter incubator

Used to produce “silk”—stable type V beta cocoa butter crystals (33.8°C) required for professional chocolate tempering.

  • Technology: EZtemper
  • Purpose: cocoa butter preparation
  • Result: consistent tempering
  • Measurement accuracy: +/- 0.1°C
  • Operating mode: continuous
  • Power consumption: 10 W
